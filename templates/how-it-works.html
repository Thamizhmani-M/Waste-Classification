How EcoSort Works

Sorting waste correctly is easier than ever. Follow our simple 6-step process to identify materials and learn how to recycle them properly.

01

Log In Safely

Start by signing into your account. Once you're in, you'll see a dashboard showing an overview of sorted items. From there, you can easily start a new scan.

02

Choose Your Method

Decide how you want to scan your items. You can choose to analyze a single Photo, upload a short Video, or use your Live Camera to scan items directly.

03

Add Your Photo or Video

Upload your picture or video file from your computer or phone. Our system will safely receive it and get it ready to be checked.

04

Automatic Scanning

Our smart system looks at your photo or video and quickly figures out what items are in it. It separates them into categories like wet waste, dry recyclables, electronics, or hazardous materials.

Done in seconds
05

See the Results

You'll get a clear, easy-to-read list of everything we found. The results will tell you exactly what the material is, whether it can be recycled, and which color bin it belongs in.

06

Get Helpful Tips

We do more than just name the items. We'll show you roughly how long it takes for them to break down in nature and give you simple instructions on the best way to recycle or dispose of them safely.
